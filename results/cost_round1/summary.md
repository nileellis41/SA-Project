# Cost round 1: freeze size at entry, vol gate on new entries

Generated by `uv run python -m analysis.experiments.cost_round1`. All results are out of sample from the walk-forward folds: 5y train, 1y test, 10-bar purge, 5-bar embargo, 0.25 bp per Treasury leg one way, 10% vol target. The cost table, folds, PCA construction, 60-bar OU window and entry bands are unchanged.

## Bottom line

- **Headline (PCA-2 + credit): still net negative at 1x.** Net Sharpe went from −0.60 (baseline) to −0.35 (freeze) and −0.22 (freeze + gate).
- **PCA-3 sparse: barely net positive at 1x.** Net Sharpe went from −0.09 to +0.07 (freeze) and +0.09 (freeze + gate), which is +0.6% and +0.7% of capital a year. That is not a result. The deflated Sharpe of the best config is 0.009 at 35 trials, so it cannot be told apart from zero. Mean net P&L per trade is also still negative (−0.10 risk units).
- **Freeze size is the clean win.** It has no fitted parameter and leaves gross P&L essentially intact (gross Sharpe 0.55→0.56, 1.21→1.17). It cuts annual cost by about 11–14% (9.6→8.2% and 10.8→9.5% of capital) and lifts net Sharpe by +0.25 (headline) and +0.16 (PCA-3 sparse).
- **The vol gate cuts cost by roughly half again, but it also cuts gross.** Gross Sharpe falls from 0.56 to 0.34 (headline) and from 1.17 to 0.56 (PCA-3 sparse), and trades per year fall from 13.1 to 8.4 and from 7.9 to 3.4. Net Sharpe improves by only +0.13 and +0.02 over freeze alone. X was fitted per member per fold on the training window (grid: training percentiles 10–50 of trailing residual vol), and **that selection counts as a trial**.

## Overlapping members: book basis vs distinct-portfolio basis

`run_sleeve1.sleeve` (src/amp_sa/run_sleeve1.py:48-57) takes an equal-weight mean over live members. Members that are the same portfolio are therefore separate positions, and a group of c copies gets c/n of the sleeve's risk. The total size is still set by the 10% vol target, so overlap moves risk between distinct portfolios; it does not lever the book up. For example, before 2010 all four PCA-3 sparse members are one 4-leg portfolio, and the book holds it once at full weight.

`merged_basis.csv` re-runs the same sleeve and vol target with each distinct portfolio given one equal share per bar. Members count as the same portfolio when their leg vectors are equal up to scale.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCA-3 sparse | baseline | −0.087 | −0.097 | 1.21 | 1.20 |
| PCA-3 sparse | freeze_size | +0.073 | +0.064 | 1.17 | 1.16 |
| PCA-3 sparse | freeze + vol_gate | +0.090 | +0.061 | 0.56 | 0.54 |
| Headline | all configs | unchanged (PCA-2 full has no duplicate members) | | | |

Overlap moves PCA-3 sparse's net Sharpe by only 0.01–0.03. The trade-table sums (+9.4 over 691 member trades, −84.8 over 255 merged trades) are unweighted totals in member risk units, and **neither one is the book's P&L**. Before 2010 every trade appears four times in the unmerged table, while the book holds that portfolio once. One caveat: copies with the same legs hold different positions on about 11% of shared bars (1,416 of 12,558 bar-groups). Their bands are fitted separately, so they are not always bit-identical trades.

## Caveats

- **PCA-3 sparse 2010–13 hedge defect: deliberately not fixed in this round.** The near-singular 2y/5y/10y hedge of the 3y member is still in every PCA-3 sparse row above. The affected folds are the four whose test windows start 2010-05-21, 2011-05-10, 2012-04-26 and 2013-04-15, so trades entered from 2010-05-21 through 2014-04-01.
  - **Baseline, merged trades** (`model_trades`; identical sparse-hedge copies such as 2y = 3y = 5y count once): 13 trades entered in those folds, net −34.1 risk units, against −84.8 over all 255 merged trades. The 7 of them that contain the 3y net −20.1.
  - **Baseline, unmerged member trades** (the basis of the deck's figure): 29 trades, net −67.5, against +9.4 over all 691 member trades.
  - **freeze_size + vol_gate, merged:** 9 trades, net −32.1. The 6 that contain the 3y net −9.7.
  - An earlier version of this note quoted "−14.8 / −4.8". Those figures were only the merged 2y = 3y = 5y trades entered in calendar 2010–13: that filter missed the stand-alone 3y trade entered 2014-03-07 (−5.2) and every trade without the 3y.
- **Credit pairs.** In the headline, the credit pairs are frozen but not vol-gated. They come from `amp_sa.sleeves.rv`, which this pipeline does not refit.
- **What "freeze" means.** The member position is held at signal/vol at entry. The sleeve weight is fixed at the trade's first held bar, which is known at the entry close. Open trades are never re-traded for leverage changes. Weights are computed exactly as in production.
- **Drawdown overlay.** It is unchanged, which means off: production `run_sleeve1.sleeve` uses `use_overlay=False`.
- **Fitting cost.** Bands and X were fitted at 1x cost. The 0.5x and 2x rows re-price the same positions without refitting.
